# Code of Conduct

## Do not distribute IPAs

Building a patched YouTube IPA is a legal grey area at best. Do **not** upload,
publish, or distribute any `.ipa` produced with this repository or a fork of it.
Google and the tweak authors can and will act on distribution. Build for yourself.

## Scope

This repository only orchestrates third-party tweaks: it compiles, downloads and
injects them. Problems with a tweak's behaviour belong in that tweak's own
repository, not here.

## Attribution

The tweaks under `Tweaks/` are third-party projects. Keep their licenses and
credits intact when you fork or redistribute this repository.
